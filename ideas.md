# BUILD A HUMAN MACHINE

    Duration: 8–10 minutes

Rules:

1. The host announces a fictional machine.
2. One participant starts by performing a repetitive physical action and making a sound.
3. The next participant joins with a different action and sound.
4. Continue until 5–8 participants become parts of one giant human machine.
5. Everyone repeats their actions together.
   Machine Challenges:
6. Monday Morning Machine
7. Automatic Salary Spending Machine
8. Office Gossip Detector
9. Manager Approval Machine
10. Coffee-to-Productivity Converter
11. Automatic Leave Rejection Machine
12. IT Support Escalation Machine
13. Deadline Panic Machine
14. Automatic Meeting Generator
15. Weekend Waiting Machine
    Example: Coffee-to-Productivity Machine

- Person 1: Pretends to pour coffee.
- Person 2: Makes a drinking sound.
- Person 3: Starts typing furiously.
- Person 4: Shouts "DEADLINE!"
- Person 5: Pretends to fall asleep.
  Everyone repeats their actions simultaneously.

Winner: The funniest and most creative human machine.

# 30-SECOND MEMORY HEIST

Duration: 7–10 minutes

Rules:

1. Display a collection of random objects on screen.
2. Participants get 30 seconds to memorize everything.
3. Hide the objects.
4. Give participants 45 seconds to write down as many objects as they remember.
5. Reveal the correct answers.
6. Award one point for every correct object.
   Rounds:

- Round 1: 15 objects.
- Round 2: 20 objects.
- Round 3: 25 objects.
  Bonus Round – Spot the Difference

1. Display 20 random objects for 20 seconds.
2. Hide the slide.
3. Secretly replace 3 objects with different objects.
4. Display the updated slide.
5. Participants must identify the 3 changed objects.
   Scoring:

- One point for each correctly remembered object.
- Two bonus points for each correctly identified changed object.
  Winner: The participant with the highest total score.

General hosting instructions:

- Keep all instructions in simple English.
- Allow participants to respond in Tamil or English.
- Avoid complicated team formations.
- Keep rounds short and energetic.
- Encourage everyone to participate rather than allowing only the fastest participants to answer.

# TANGKHUL LANGUAGE CHALLENGE – CAN YOU SPEAK TANGKHUL?

Duration: 5–7 minutes

Objective: Introduce colleagues to the Tangkhul language through a fun guessing and pronunciation game.

Rules:

1. Display a Tangkhul word or phrase on the screen.
2. Participants must guess its meaning.
3. Provide three possible answers (A, B, C).
4. Reveal the correct English meaning.
5. Ask participants to pronounce the word.
6. Award points for correct answers and pronunciation attempts.
   Rounds:

- Round 1: 5 easy everyday words.
- Round 2: 5 commonly used phrases.
  Bonus Challenge: Ask participants to use a newly learned Tangkhul word in a funny English sentence.

Scoring:

- Correct meaning: 2 points.
- Good pronunciation: 2 points.
- Funniest sentence: 3 bonus points.

# TANGKHUL vs TAMIL – LANGUAGE COMPARISON CHALLENGE

Duration: 5–7 minutes

Objective: Explore similarities and differences between Tangkhul and Tamil through an entertaining language challenge.

Rules:

1. Display a Tangkhul word on the screen.
2. Ask participants to guess its meaning.
3. Give three possible English meanings.
4. Reveal the correct answer.
5. If there is a genuine similarity with a Tamil word, explain the comparison.
6. Let participants discuss whether the words sound similar.
   Rounds:

- Round 1: Everyday words.
- Round 2: Food and household words.
- Round 3: Common expressions.
- Round 4: Funny or surprising words.
  Bonus Challenge: Ask Tamil-speaking participants to teach you one Tamil word that sounds interesting or has an amusing meaning.

Scoring:

- Correct guess: 2 points.
- Correct pronunciation: 2 points.
- Best language comparison: 3 bonus points.

# TANGKHUL SURVIVAL TEST

Duration: 8–10 minutes

Objective: Test how well participants can communicate in Tangkhul through funny real-life situations.

Rules:

1. Display a funny real-life situation.
2. Participants must figure out what they would say in Tangkhul.
3. Give them three possible phrases or translations.
4. Reveal the correct phrase and its English meaning.
5. Ask participants to pronounce it.
6. Introduce an unexpected twist to make the situation funnier.
   Scenarios:

Round 1: Lost in Ukhrul

You are visiting Ukhrul for the first time and have no idea where you're going. You need to ask someone for directions.

Round 2: Hungry in a Tangkhul Village

You are visiting a Tangkhul village, and you're extremely hungry. You need to ask someone for food.

Round 3: Meeting Someone for the First Time

You meet a Tangkhul person and want to introduce yourself and ask how they are doing.

Round 4: Someone Invites You for a Meal

Someone invites you to eat, but you don't understand what they're saying. What would you respond?

Round 5: You Accidentally Offend Someone

You accidentally say something inappropriate and need to apologize.

Bonus Challenge: Give participants a situation without providing any options. Let them attempt to communicate using the Tangkhul words they've learned.

Scoring:

- Correct phrase: 2 points.
- Correct pronunciation: 2 points.
- Most entertaining attempt: 3 bonus points.

Host preparation note: Prepare the actual Tangkhul words, phrases, English meanings and pronunciations beforehand, preferably using your own dialect. This ensures linguistic accuracy while allowing you to run the games directly from your presentation.
